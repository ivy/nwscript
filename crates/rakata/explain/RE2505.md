A surface material table without a `walk` column, or with a `walk` cell that is not a number.

Whether a walkmesh face is walkable is the `walk` column of `surfacemat.2da`, in the row the face's material number names: 1 walkable, 0 not, a blank cell not. The table read has no column named `walk` (the name is case-sensitive), holds a cell there that is not an integer, or does not read as a 2DA at all.

```text
error[RE2505]: surface material table: row 3's `walk` cell "yes" is not a number
```

Read the table the install's resolution order gives for `surfacemat.2da` (`data/2da.bif` in the vanilla game) and check the column and cell the message names.
