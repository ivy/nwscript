A mip level the texture does not have.

A TPC stores each image with a chain of mip levels, level 0 the largest and each next one half its size; `rakata texture show` lists them. `rakata texture export --level N`, `Tpc::decode_level` and `Tpc::decode_image` were asked for a level past the last one the texture stores. A TGA has one level, level 0.

```text
error[RE2210]: mip level 12 asked for; the texture has 10, 0 to 9
```

Ask for a level the texture has, or use `--all-levels` to export every one.
