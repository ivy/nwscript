A TGA image type or pixel depth that is not decoded.

This crate decodes TGA image types 2 (true colour) and 3 (greyscale), raw, and 10 and 11, their run-length encoded forms: true colour at 16, 24 or 32 bits per pixel, greyscale at 8, or 16 with alpha. Every TGA in the KotOR 1 install is one of these. The file read is colour-mapped (types 1 and 9), has no image (type 0), or has a pixel depth its type does not take.

```text
error[RE2205]: TGA image type 1 at 8 bits per pixel is not decoded; types 2, 3, 10 and 11 are
```

Save the image again as 24- or 32-bit true colour, which the game reads too.
