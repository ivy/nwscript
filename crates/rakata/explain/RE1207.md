A party member named with `--party` has no `AVAILNPC<n>.utc` in the save.

The engine builds a party member from the creature the save holds for that NPC number, `AVAILNPC<n>.utc` inside `SAVEGAME.sav`; there is one for each NPC the player has met. A member is named by its NPC number (`2`) or by that creature's tag (`carth`), compared without regard to case.

```text
error[RE1207]: the save holds no AVAILNPC for party member `bastila`; it holds 1 (t3m4), 2 (carth), 6 (mission)
```

The message lists the NPCs the save holds; `rakata save show DIR` shows the current party.
