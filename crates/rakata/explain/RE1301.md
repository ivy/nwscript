An archive member the archive does not hold.

A name of the form `ARCHIVE!MEMBER` reads one member of an archive: an ERF-family file (`.erf`, `.mod`, `.sav`, `.hak`) or a RIM by the member's `name.ext`, or a BIF by its position, since a BIF holds no names. The archive was read, and no member answers to the name.

```text
error[RE1301]: `modules/danm14ac.mod` holds no member `dan14_juhan.utc`
error[RE1301]: `data/2da.bif` holds no member `appearance.2da`
  = help: a BIF holds no names: name a member by position, as `#N` or `#N.ext`
```

Check the spelling and the extension; names compare without regard to case. `rakata which NAME.EXT --game DIR` lists every copy the install holds and where; a BIF member's position is the number it shows in brackets. A KEY holds no resources' bytes; name the BIF it indexes.
