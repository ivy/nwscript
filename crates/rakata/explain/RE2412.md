The model a supermodel chain starts from is not found.

Loading a model's supermodel chain begins by asking the loader for the model itself, and the loader has no model of that name.

```text
error[RE2412]: model `s_mael01` is not found
```

Check the name. A missing supermodel further up the chain is not an error: the chain stops there and names it, as it does for the three vanilla models whose supermodel the game does not ship.
