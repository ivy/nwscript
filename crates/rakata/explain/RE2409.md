A skin whose bone map, qbones and tbones disagree.

A skin mesh binds its vertices to bones. Its bone map holds one float per node number: the node's local bone index, or -1 when the node is no bone of the mesh; the vertices name bones by local index. The qbones and tbones hold a bind rotation and translation per node number. The skin named has a bone map entry that is not a whole number, two nodes with the same local index, a local index no node has, or a bone map, qbone array and tbone array of different lengths.

```text
error[RE2409]: skin `torso`: two nodes have bone index 3
```

The model is damaged. In every vanilla skin the bone map names local indices 0, 1, 2, … each once, and the three arrays have one entry per node number.
