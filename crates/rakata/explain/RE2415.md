A model whose parts disagree, so the writer cannot lay it out.

The writer derives every offset and count from the model's parts, and some parts must agree with fields the model keeps: a mesh's positions, its dangly positions and its saber arrays hold one entry per vertex of the vertex count in its mesh header; its MDX block holds the vertex count times the stride, and its terminator one stride; its index lists match its index counts; an AABB entry names entries of its own tree; the model header's node is one of the geometry's. One of these does not hold.

```text
error[RE2415]: the model's parts disagree: a mesh of 24 vertices has 276 bytes of positions, not 12 per vertex
```

Change the part and the field together: the vertex count lives at offset 304 of the mesh header.
