No layer of the install holds the resource asked for.

The search runs in the engine's order: `Override/`, then the capsules of the module named with `--module` (`.mod` over `.rim` over `_s.rim`, then the module's lip-sync pack under `lips/`), then KotOR 1's `patch.erf`, then the texture packs (the quality pack `--texture-pack` selects, then `TexturePacks/swpc_tex_gui.erf`), then the BIFs `chitin.key` indexes. A resource that is in none of them is not in the install, or lives in a module that was not named.

```text
error[RE0404]: no `k_pdan_juhani31.ncs` in Override, the texture packs or the game's own files; a module's copy needs its module named
```

Most scripts, dialogues and blueprints ship inside a module's capsules and are found only with `--module STEM`. A texture only the unselected quality packs hold is not searched, nor are sounds and music under the stream folders and the menu archives under `rims/`, which the engine loads by other means. A texture's TXI is often not a resource of its own: the TPCs in the packs carry it as text after the pixels, so `fnt_d16x16.txi` is in no layer though `fnt_d16x16.tpc` is.
