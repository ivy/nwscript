No layer of the install holds the resource asked for.

The search runs in the engine's order: `Override/`, then the capsules of the module named with `--module` (`.mod` over `.rim` over `_s.rim`, then the module's lip-sync pack under `lips/`), then KotOR 1's `patch.erf`, then the BIFs `chitin.key` indexes. A resource that is in none of them is not in the install, or lives in a module that was not named.

```text
error[RE0404]: no `k_pdan_juhani31.ncs` in Override or the game's own files; a module's copy needs its module named
```

Most scripts, dialogues and blueprints ship inside a module's capsules and are found only with `--module STEM`. Textures in `TexturePacks/`, sounds and music under the stream folders, and the menu archives under `rims/` are loaded by the engine by other means and are not searched.
