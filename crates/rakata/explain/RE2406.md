A mesh's vertex layout that does not fit its stride.

A mesh's vertices in the MDX are interleaved: each vertex is `stride` bytes, and the mesh header gives each attribute's offset within it (positions, normals, two sets of texture coordinates), or -1 when the vertex has none. The mesh named gives an attribute whose floats run past the stride, or gives attributes in the MDX and no MDX offset for its block.

```text
error[RE2406]: mesh `floor`'s vertex layout: an attribute of 2 floats at 36 passes the stride of 40
```

The mesh header is damaged, or the model was written by a tool that does not produce the game's layout.
