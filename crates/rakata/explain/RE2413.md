Models given as a supermodel chain out of the order their supermodels name.

A lineage takes a model and its supermodels in order: each model followed by the supermodel it names. The model named is followed by a different model, or names no supermodel and is followed by one.

```text
error[RE2413]: model `S_Female02` names supermodel `S_Female01` and is followed by `S_Male02`
```

Names compare without case, against the name in each model's header. Loading the chain with the crate's loader gives the models in order.
