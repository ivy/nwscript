A texture pack named by something other than `tpa`, `tpb` or `tpc`.

The texture layer searches one of the three quality packs under `TexturePacks/` (`swpc_tex_tpa.erf`, `swpc_tex_tpb.erf`, `swpc_tex_tpc.erf`), then `swpc_tex_gui.erf`. A pack is named by its short name or its file name, in any case.

```text
error[RE0405]: `tpd` is not a texture pack: tpa, tpb or tpc
```

Name one of the three. Which of them the game's `Texture Quality` option selects is not established, so `tpa`, the largest textures, is the default.
