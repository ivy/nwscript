A cube face or animation frame the texture does not have.

A TPC holds one image, six for a cube map, or `numx × numy` frames for an animation sheet; `rakata texture show` says which. `rakata texture export --image N`, `Tpc::decode_image` and `Tpc::to_pngs` were asked for an image past the last one: any image but 0 of a single-image texture, a face past 5 of a cube, a frame past the sheet's count.

```text
error[RE2211]: image 6 asked for; the texture has 6, 0 to 5
```

Ask for an image the texture has, or leave out `--image` to export the faces or frames laid out together.
