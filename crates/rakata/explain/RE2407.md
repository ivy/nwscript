A face or triangle list entry naming a vertex the mesh does not have.

Each face of a mesh names three vertices by index, and the mesh's triangle list repeats those indices for drawing. An index of the mesh named is the vertex count or more, so a renderer reading it would read past the mesh's vertices.

```text
error[RE2407]: mesh `floor` names vertex 412 and has 400 vertices
```

The model is damaged. Every vanilla mesh's faces and triangle list name only its own vertices.
