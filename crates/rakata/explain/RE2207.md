A TPC the writer cannot write as given.

The writer puts the header's fields at their offsets, the pixel data after the 128-byte header, and the TXI after the pixels. The header bytes after the fields it names (offsets 14 to 127) are kept as `reserved`; the TPC given holds more of them than those 114 bytes, so the header could not hold them and the pixels would not start at offset 128.

```text
error[RE2207]: cannot write the TPC: 120 reserved header bytes where the header has room for 114
```

Give `reserved` at most 114 bytes; fewer are padded with zeros.
