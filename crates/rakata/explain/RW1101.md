A link to a node the dialogue does not have.

A starting link, or a link in an entry's `RepliesList` or a reply's `EntriesList`, carries an `Index` past the end of the list it points into. The engine has no node to show there. The transcript prints the link as `-> R12 (no such node)` and goes on.

```text
warning[RW1101]: broken.dlg links to R12, past the end of its 30 entries and 12 replies
```

A tool that removed nodes without renumbering the links is the usual cause.
