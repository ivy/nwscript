A `.tpc` in Override beside a `.tga` or `.dds` of the same name.

A texture held both as a `.tpc` and as a `.tga` or `.dds` in `Override/` crashes the game when it is loaded. Mods ship textures in every format, and two mods that replace one texture in different formats leave both.

```text
error[RE1901]: `Override/lhr_flr01.tpc` lies beside `lhr_flr01.tga` in Override: the game crashes on a texture held as both
```

Remove one; modshop's `dedupe_tpc` step removes the `.tpc`. This is an error because it has crashed the game; the other tree lints are warnings.
