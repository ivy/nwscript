A BIF member index past the end of its table.

A BIF's members have no names; the KEY names them by position, and so does a replace into a BIF. The position given is at or past the number of members the BIF holds.

```text
error[RE0209]: the BIF has no member 6444; it holds 6444
```

When the index came from a KEY entry, the KEY and the BIF disagree: one was replaced without the other, which is how a partial patch leaves an install. When it is a position given by hand, it is off by one or meant for another BIF.
