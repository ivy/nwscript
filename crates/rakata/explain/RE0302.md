A `Key=Value` entry before the first `[Section]` header of a change file.

Every entry of a `changes.ini` belongs to a section: `[Settings]`, one of the lists, or a section a list entry names. An entry above the first header belongs to none, so TSLPatcher never reads it and `rakata` reports it.

```text
error[RE0302]: `LogLevel` comes before any `[Section]` header
```

Move the entry under the section it was meant for; `[Settings]` holds the patcher's own options.
