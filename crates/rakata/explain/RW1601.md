A resource that does not read as its format, compared by hash.

The diff compares each pair of files or members whose bytes differ by its format, chosen by extension: GFF by field path, 2DA by row and column, the talk table by strref, a dialogue by node, a LIP by keyframe, an archive by member. One side of this pair did not read as that format, so its contents were not compared: the entry shows the two hashes and is marked "contents not compared", and the diff goes on.

```text
warning[RW1601]: `Override/broken.utc` does not read as gff: B: RE0602: ...; compared by hash
```

The message names the side (A or B) and the reader's own error, whose code `rakata explain` covers. A file a mod tool wrote that the engine accepts and the reader refuses is a defect in the reader; `rakata conformance` over the tree shows every such file.
