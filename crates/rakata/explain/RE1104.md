A node key that does not parse.

A key is `voice:RESREF`, `strref:N`, `text:TEXT`, `entry:N` or `reply:N`, or an index written `E3` or `R0`. A voice resref is at most sixteen bytes; a strref and an index are numbers.

```text
error[RE1104]: cannot parse the node key `strref:abc`: a strref is a number
```
