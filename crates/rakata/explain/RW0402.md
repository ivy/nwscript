The selected quality pack is not under `TexturePacks/`.

The texture layer searches the quality pack the caller selects (`tpa` unless `--texture-pack` names another), then `swpc_tex_gui.erf`. When the folder lacks the selected pack, only the GUI pack is searched, and an area or creature texture the pack would hold resolves nowhere or to a copy further down the order.

```text
warning[RW0402]: the texture pack `TexturePacks/swpc_tex_tpb.erf` is not in the install: the texture layer holds only swpc_tex_gui.erf
```

Select a pack the install holds, or restore the missing one from the game's distribution.
