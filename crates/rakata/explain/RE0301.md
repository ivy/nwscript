A line of a change file that is neither a `[Section]` header, a `Key=Value` entry, a `;` comment nor blank.

TSLPatcher reads `changes.ini` and `namespaces.ini` as Windows INI files: a header is `[` to `]` on its own line, an entry has `=` in it, and a comment starts with `;`. A line in any other form, such as a `//` comment, a header without its `]`, or prose, is nothing the patcher reads, so `rakata` reports the line and reads on without it.

```text
error[RE0301]: neither a section header nor `Key=Value`: `// set the appearance`
```

Make the line a comment with `;`, or give it the `Key=Value` or `[Section]` shape it was meant to have.
