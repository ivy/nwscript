A write naming a journal entry its category lacks.

A dialogue node's `Quest` and `QuestEntry`, or a script's `AddJournalQuestEntry(TAG, ID)` with literal arguments, names an entry the category of `global.jrl` tagged TAG does not have. The engine finds no entry of that id, so the journal shows nothing new.

```text
warning[RW2102]: mymod_guide.dlg R4 (mymod.mod) writes entry 70 of the quest `mymod_quest`, which global.jrl's category lacks
```

Either the entry's id is wrong where it is written, or the journal lacks the entry (an edit to `global.jrl` that did not land, or one the mod meant to append).
