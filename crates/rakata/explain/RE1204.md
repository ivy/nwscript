No creature in the places searched carries the tag asked for.

`rakata save show DIR --creature TAG` looks in the module given by `--module` (its GIT's `Creature List` and its IFO's player list), or in every module the save holds, and in the party members' `AVAILNPC<n>.utc`. Tags compare without regard to case. A creature that has left the area, or was destroyed, is in none of them; a party member is in its `AVAILNPC` whatever the module.

```text
error[RE1204]: no creature tagged `bastila` in danm14ac or the party members
```

Run `rakata save show DIR --module M` to list the creatures the module holds, with their tags as saved.
