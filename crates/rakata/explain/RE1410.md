A voice line whose span its take's transcript does not hold.

A voice line is a stretch of a recording (a take): its transcript's segments `first` to `last`, counted from 0, or the whole take. The line names a take the call was not given, or segments the transcript does not have.

```text
error[RE1410]: the voice line oaj_juh01: juhani_take1 has segments 0..2, not 0..7
```

Check the line's take and segment numbers against the take's transcript.
