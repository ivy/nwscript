A GFF label or resref longer than sixteen bytes.

A field's label and a `CResRef` value are each at most sixteen bytes, the width of their fields in the file. The text given is longer, or a file's resref carries a size byte above sixteen.

```text
error[RE0608]: the label `TransitionDestination` is 21 bytes; sixteen is the most
```

BioWare abbreviates (`TransitionDestin`); do the same. For a resref, the engine's names are at most sixteen characters too, so a longer one names no resource it could load.
