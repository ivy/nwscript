A model too large for the format's offsets and counts.

The MDL stores offsets into its model data and its MDX, and the counts of its arrays, as 32-bit words. The model given would need an offset or a count past what a word holds.

```text
error[RE2416]: the model does not fit the format: an offset in the MDX
```

The largest vanilla MDL is under 5 MB and the largest MDX under 8 MB.
