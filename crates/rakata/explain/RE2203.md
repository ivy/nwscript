A TPC header describing no layout of images and mip levels.

A TPC's header gives a width, a height and a mip count, and those fix how the pixel data divides into images and levels. The header read cannot be laid out: the width or height is zero, the mip count is zero, or the data-size field is larger than level 0 with one mip level, which marks an animation sheet, and the TXI after the pixels does not give `numx` and `numy` that divide the image into frames whose mip chains fill exactly that many bytes.

```text
error[RE2203]: TPC layout: 4 frames of 128×128 with 8 levels take 87488 bytes, the data-size field says 87808
```

For an animation sheet, check the TXI's `numx` and `numy` against the image: the frames are `width / numx` by `height / numy`, each stored with a full mip chain to 1×1. Otherwise the header is damaged; re-extract the file.
