A global `globalcat.2da` does not declare.

The engine keeps a global only when `globalcat.2da` declares its name and type: reading any other name answers 0 and setting it does nothing. A mod that reads or sets one of the game's globals, or one of its own, needs its row, and its type decides the routine (`GetGlobalBoolean` or `GetGlobalNumber`).

```text
error[RE1417]: globalcat.2da does not declare CN_UNDECLARED; the engine keeps no such global, reading it answers 0 and setting it does nothing
```

Declare the global (the authoring API's `globals::declare`) before asking for its type, or correct its name.
