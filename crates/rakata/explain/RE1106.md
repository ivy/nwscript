A link or start that cannot be made: between two nodes of one kind, a start into a reply, or at a position past the end of its list.

A conversation alternates: an entry (a line of the owner's or another speaker's) links to replies, and a reply (the player's line) links to entries. The starting list leads into entries only. A position names a place among a node's links or among the starts, from 0 up to the number already there, which appends.

