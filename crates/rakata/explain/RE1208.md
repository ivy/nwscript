The party given with `--party` is one the game cannot take: a member is named twice, or more than two are named.

The player travels with at most two party members. `--party` lists them in order, by NPC number or tag; `--party ''` leaves the player alone.

```text
error[RE1208]: the party cannot be written: 3 members named; the game takes at most 2 beside the player
```
