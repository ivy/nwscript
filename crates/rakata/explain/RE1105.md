A dialogue that no global layer holds and several modules do, with no module named.

Without `--module`, `rakata dialog` looks for `NAME.dlg` in `Override/`, `patch.erf` and the BIFs, then in every module's capsules. When exactly one module holds it, that module is used and its scripts are searched too. When several do, which copy is meant is the user's choice: the copies may differ, and each module's scripts are its own.

```text
error[RE1105]: k_hen_dialog.dlg is in no global layer and in 2 modules: danm13, danm14aa; name one with --module
```

Pass `--module STEM` with one of the modules named.
