An instruction has the wrong number of operands, or an operand is not the kind the instruction takes.

Each instruction's operands are fixed by its layout: `CONSTI` takes one 32-bit integer, `CPDOWNSP` an offset and a size, `ACTION` a routine number and an argument count, `DESTRUCT` three sizes, jumps a label or a signed relative offset such as `+8`. Integers may be decimal or `0x` hexadecimal. Floats are decimal (`1.5`, `-0.0`, `inf`) or `bits(0x7fc00000)` for an exact bit pattern. Strings are quoted, with `\"`, `\\`, `\n`, `\r`, `\t` and `\xNN` escapes, and the rest of the line must be ASCII.

```text
    CPDOWNSP -8          ; error[E0111]: `CPDOWNSP` takes 2 operands, found 1
    CPDOWNSP -8, 4       ; fixed
```
