```text
$ nws --game k1 check k_my_script.nss
error[E0002]: no nwscript.nss found
```

```text
$ nws --game k1 --nwscript ~/kotor/nwscript.nss check k_my_script.nss
```

KotOR's `nwscript.nss` is in `scripts.bif`; a corpus written by `cargo xtask corpus extract` has it at its root.
