A number literal is not in a form NWScript reads.

Integers are decimal digits such as `42`, or hexadecimal with a lowercase `0x` prefix such as `0x2a`. Floats have digits on both sides of the point and an optional `f` suffix: `1.0`, `0.5f`. Forms that C accepts but the corpus never uses, such as `1.`, `.5`, `1e3` or `1.0F`, are not accepted, and a number may not run straight into letters, as in `10seconds`.

```text
float fDelay = 2.;
```

Write the number in one of the forms above: `2.0` here.
